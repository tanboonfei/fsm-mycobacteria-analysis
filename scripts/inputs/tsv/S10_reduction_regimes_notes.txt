Note. Phylogenetic independence of FSM and the leprosy clade. They lie on opposite sides of the deepest split in the ingroup, and their divergence is comparable to that between slow and rapid growers in general (median patristic distance 0.87 versus 0.74 substitutions per site). Both are nested among large-genome relatives: the FSM sister group has a median genome size of 5.82 Mb, and the leprosy clade is sister to M. haemophilum (4.24 Mb) within a slow-growing clade whose other members have a median of 5.94 Mb. The two reductions therefore represent separate origins that can be analysed under a single tree and codon model. Synonymous divergence between them is substantial, which permits model fitting but limits the interpretation of branch-specific dS.

Note. Annotation-pipeline dependence of the leprosy-clade metrics. Prodigal and PGAP coding densities differ by 12–16 percentage points for the three decayed genomes, compared with 0.24 points between Prodigal and Bakta for M. leprae; the difference corresponds to the fraction of the genome that PGAP assigns to pseudogenes. Bakta predicts 3,984 CDS in M. leprae, a genome with approximately 1,600 intact genes. Both counts are reported (Table S6); Fig. 2b uses Bakta throughout.
