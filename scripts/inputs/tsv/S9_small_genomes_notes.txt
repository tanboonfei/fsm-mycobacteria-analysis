Note. Three-panel block analysis. Under the three-panel analysis (Table S9f), the 59 raw-size non-FSM genomes below 4 Mb comprise 7 cultured isolates or closed assemblies, 17 SPIRE MAGs and 35 GTDB reference MAGs. Among cultured isolates and closed assemblies alone (276 genomes), the 7 small genomes form 5 blocks, the largest being the three-genome leprosy clade. With every MAG scored by CheckM2 v1.0.1, sizes corrected for completeness and MAGs below 90% pruned (750 tips), 33 small genomes outside FSM form 20 blocks, none larger than three and three with internal divergence above 0.05 substitutions per site; the 33 comprise 7 isolates, 16 SPIRE MAGs and 10 reference MAGs. Of the original 59, 23 are MAGs below 90% completeness and 3 exceed 4 Mb once corrected. FSM contributes 96 small genomes of the 115 in that panel, with a largest block of 20; at 3.5 and 4.5 Mb the corrected analysis gives 9 and 32 non-FSM blocks with largest blocks of 3 and 6, against FSM largest blocks of 15 and 49. The count of independent reductions outside FSM is therefore sensitive to assembly completeness (43 by raw size, 20 corrected, 5 among isolates); the largest non-FSM block is 3 under every panel at 3.5 and 4 Mb.
