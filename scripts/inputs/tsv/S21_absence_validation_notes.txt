Note. Profiles were built from the RGM orthologues of each gene (one per genome, taken from the Bakta product annotation), aligned with MAFFT and converted to an HMM with hmmbuild; hmmsearch was then run against all 95 FSM MAG proteomes and, independently of gene calling, against a six-frame translation of the FSM contigs (2,999,389 open reading frames of 60 amino acids or more). The score cut for each gene is half the median best bitscore of the top 50 RGM genomes, an empirical threshold that separates true orthologues from the paralogous background; kdpB in particular recovers several hundred P-type ATPase paralogues per clade at any E-value threshold, which is why the calls are made on bitscore. The profile search recovers the same picture as the single-query tBLASTn of Table S21 for every gene except kdpB, where the six-frame search was not run, and the absence calls do not depend on the choice of a single query protein. RGM prevalences from the profiles (ureC 84%, ectC 59%, catA 27%, narG 44%, kdpB 71%) differ slightly from the Bakta product-name prevalences of Table S17 for catA, narG and kdpB, because the profile detects more divergent homologues than a product-name match.
