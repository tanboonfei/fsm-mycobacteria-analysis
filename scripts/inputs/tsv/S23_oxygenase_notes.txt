Notes. reference_class describes what the nearest reference does, not what the FSM enzyme does; at 26-40% identity the nearest reference constrains neither substrate nor cleavage position. The Synechocystis ACO is classed here as retinal-producing because it yields retinal from apocarotenoid substrates, not from beta-carotene; the source FASTA labels it NONretinal on the latter basis, and the two labels describe different substrates rather than disagreeing. The separate protein phylogeny in the oxygenase-tree directory of the analysis code is built on PF03055 members only: Blh and Brp are not members of that family and return no hit against its profile. That tree cannot place them and no absence of support for the bacterial retinal producers should be read from it.
