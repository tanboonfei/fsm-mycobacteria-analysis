Note. OLS of the number of the 120 polymorphic families retained per genome (n = 57, R2 = 0.671). Completeness is not significant on its own but is significant once genome size is controlled; retention is independent of completeness only marginally and not in the joint model. N50 and contig density, the observable consequences of sequencing depth, do not improve the fit further. SPIRE does not distribute per-MAG read depth and the assembled contigs carry no coverage field; these are the available proxies. Family counts differ slightly from Table S14 (1,072/120/53 against 1,067/125/53) because completeness here is this study's CheckM2 v1.0.1 value rather than the SPIRE value.
